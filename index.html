My Japanese curry recipe

I originally decided to make this after seeing it in an anime. I made it for my mother and grandmother with pork the first time I made it and it went over well. They liked it well enough that I made it for family dinner with my cousins and aunt shortly after. The eight of them enjoyed it enough that I started making it more often. At one point I noticed the meat wasn't as tender as I'd like and so I used a marinade recipe I use for karaage. I think it makes the meat tender and juicy while adding flavor that cuts through the richness of the sauce.

Curry Ingredients

  • 1 lb of pork, chopped
  • 2 medium onions, diced or chunked
  • 1/2 carrot, chopped
  • 1 medium potato
  • 2 1/4 cups of dashi stock (optionally sub beef)
  • 1 box curry roux
  • oil for pan

Marinade Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 3 tbsp worcestshire
  • 3 tbsp mirin
  • 3 tbsp rice wine vinegar
  • 2-3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp ginger

Steps

  1. Marinate meat for 30 minutes to an hour, up to overnight. Drain before cooking.
  2. Add meat to a hot oiled pan over medium-high heat and brown one side.
  3. Once browned, add vegetables, bring back to heat then stir fry for five minutes.
  4. Add Dashi stock and bring to a boil. Reduce to a simmer and cover. Cook for ~15 minutes.
  5. Remove heat to low and add broken up curry roux. Stir for 5 minutes.
  6. Serve alongside rice or noodle.
